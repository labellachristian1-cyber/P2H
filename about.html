About Us

Prepared to show up, every time.

We're a volunteer-run charity dedicated to making sure our neighbors have food, clothing, shelter, and a real shot at their future.

Our Mission

The Mission of Prepared 2 Help (P2H) is to support members of our community facing hardship by delivering essential resources such as food, clothing, and educational resources designed to promote self-sufficiency. P2H carry out our mission in a manner consistent with the principles of scouting, and offer youth and community members opportunities in leadership, volunteerism, character building, and service to others.

Our Story

Why we started.

After years of success with the ThanksforGiving Food Drive, Tom Mercaldo & Todd Munn looked to expand their efforts. Thus, in late 2023 Tom and Todd formally established the Perpared2Help non-profit organization, along with 3 other Board Members: Mike Olson, Sherry Felisko, & Kathy Wilson. Today, that same spirit drives our Thanks for Giving Food Drive, our three annual scholarships, and our ongoing support of local food, clothing, and shelter organizations.
