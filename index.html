What We Do

Programs built around food, education, and shelter.

Thanks for Giving Food Drive

Our annual holiday-season food drive stocks local pantries so families across the community sit down to a full table.

Nick Felisko Youth Scholarship

Awarded each year to a local student in Nick's memory, honoring his spirit of generosity and community.

Dare to Soar Scholarship

Supporting a student who has shown the courage to pursue their goals despite real obstacles along the way.

Grit Scholarship

Recognizing a student whose perseverance and determination set an example for the whole community.
